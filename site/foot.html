Titles come from every publisher catalog posted to Edelweiss in the past year, Publishers Weekly's reviews (with starred reviews flagged), Book Riot's public posts (each book links to the posts that mention it), and the coming-soon pages of the publishers on your Book Index Sources sheet. Books are matched across sources by ISBN, or by title and author. Books known only from a PW review show their month until an exact date turns up. Covers, descriptions, formats and categories are the publishers' own Edelweiss data. Genres come from each book's first BISAC category, and LGBTQ+ reads are books with an LGBTQ+ BISAC category. “In the news” means a headline in The Daily Roundup's book beats names the title and its author.
